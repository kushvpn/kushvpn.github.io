Privacy Policy – Kush VPN

Last updated: 2026-11-24

Kush VPN ("the app") provides a VPN connection using the WireGuard protocol.

What we do not collect

  • We do not log the websites you visit, DNS queries, or the content of your traffic.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number.
  • The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or trackers.

What is stored

  • On your device: a random device identifier, your WireGuard key pair and the connection settings are kept in the device's secure storage. Your private key never leaves your device.
  • On the server (registration): when you first connect, the app sends a random device identifier and your WireGuard public key. We store these together with the private VPN address assigned to you and the time of registration. Your private key is generated on your device and is never sent to us. Registrations that stay unused for 30 days are deleted automatically.
  • Registration requests: our registration server does not keep access logs of the IP addresses that contact it.
  • On the server (connection): WireGuard necessarily keeps the most recent connection time and the source IP of each active peer in memory while you are connected. This is not written to disk by us and is cleared when the server restarts.

Permissions

The app uses Android's VPN permission (BINDVPNSERVICE) solely to route your device's traffic through the Kush VPN server. Traffic is not inspected, modified, or sold.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share personal data with third parties. We may disclose information only if legally required.

Security

Connections are encrypted with WireGuard (ChaCha20-Poly1305).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
